Title: Purchase Approval Thresholds - Procedure
Document ID: PRC-0027
Department: Procurement
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.0
Effective date: 2023-02-14
Status: Active
Owner: Procurement Office
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: FIN-0055, FIN-0039, PRC-0007

Purpose
This quick reference lists the approval level needed for each spend amount.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Up to 10000 EGP: line manager.
- 10000 to 50000 EGP: department head.
- Over 50000 EGP: Procurement Committee and CFO.
