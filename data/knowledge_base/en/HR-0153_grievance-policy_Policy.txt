1. Purpose
This policy explains how to raise a formal complaint at work.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Raise the issue informally with the manager first where possible.
- A formal grievance is submitted in writing and acknowledged within 5 working days.
- A hearing is held within 15 working days, and the outcome is given in writing.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
