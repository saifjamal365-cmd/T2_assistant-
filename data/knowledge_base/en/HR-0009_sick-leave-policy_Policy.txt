1. Purpose
This policy explains sick-leave entitlement and when a medical report is needed.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Employees may take up to 10 paid sick days per year.
- A medical report is required for any sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days.
- The employee must notify their manager on the first day of absence.
- Unused sick days do not carry over and are not paid out.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.
