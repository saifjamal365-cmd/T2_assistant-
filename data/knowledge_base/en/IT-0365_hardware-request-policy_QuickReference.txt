Quick reference - Hardware Request - Quick Reference (Cairo Office).

Key points
- Standard equipment is requested through the asset portal with a business justification.
- Standard requests are approved by the manager and fulfilled within 3 working days.
- Non-standard items also need IT approval and may take longer.
