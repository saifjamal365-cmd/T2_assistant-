Quick reference - Parental Leave - Quick Reference (Jeddah Office).

Key points
- The primary caregiver receives 45 paid days; the secondary caregiver receives 5 paid days.
- Requests should reach HR at least 15 days before the expected start date with supporting documents.
- Leave may be split into two blocks within the first year after the birth or adoption.
- Job protection applies: the employee returns to the same or an equivalent role.
