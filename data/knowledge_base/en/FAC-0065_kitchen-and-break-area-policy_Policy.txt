Title: Kitchen and Break Area Policy

Type: Policy
Version: 3.2
Effective date: 2024-05-16
Status: Active

Applies to: All employees

1. Purpose
This policy covers shared kitchens and break areas.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Clean up after yourself; label personal food with your name and the date.
- The fridge is emptied every Thursday; anything unlabelled is thrown away.
- Report broken appliances to facilities.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Facilities Management.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fac@t2.example.
