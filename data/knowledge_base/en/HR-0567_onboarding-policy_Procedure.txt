Document ID: HR-0567
Department: Human Resources
Type: Procedure
Version: 2.3
Effective date: 2023-05-05
Status: Active
Owner: HR Operations
Applies to: Dubai Office employees
Related documents: HR-0079, HR-0143, IT-0023

Purpose
This policy lists what must happen when a new employee joins.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Before day one, HR sends a welcome email with the start date, location and documents to bring.
- On day one, the manager introduces the team and confirms system access is active.
- Within the first 2 weeks, the employee completes mandatory training on conduct, privacy and security.
