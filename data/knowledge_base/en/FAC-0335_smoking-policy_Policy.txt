1. Purpose
This policy covers where smoking is allowed on company premises.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Dubai Office employees.

3. Policy
- Smoking, including e-cigarettes, is allowed only in designated outdoor areas.
- Designated areas are at least 15 metres from any building entrance.
- Smoking anywhere else on the premises, including company vehicles, is not permitted.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Facilities Management.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fac@t2.example.
