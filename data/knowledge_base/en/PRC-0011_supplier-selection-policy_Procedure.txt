Title: Supplier Selection - Procedure

Department: Procurement
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.1
Effective date: 2024-07-14
Status: Active
Owner: Procurement Office
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: PRC-0007, FIN-0039, LEG-0023

Purpose
This policy explains how suppliers are chosen fairly.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Selection is based on written criteria: price, quality, delivery, and risk.
- For spend over 25000 AED, at least 3 suppliers are invited to quote.
- A conflict of interest with a supplier is declared and the person steps out of the decision.
