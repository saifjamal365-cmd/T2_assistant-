Title: Payroll Bank Account Change - Procedure
Document ID: FIN-0107
Department: Finance
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.2
Effective date: 2024-08-07
Status: Active
Owner: Finance Shared Services
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: FIN-0071, FIN-0079

Purpose
This policy covers updating the bank account salary is paid into.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- A bank account change is submitted through the HR system with a bank letter or void cheque.
- Changes submitted after the 25 of the month apply from the following month's payroll.
- Payroll will call the employee directly to confirm any account change before paying it.
