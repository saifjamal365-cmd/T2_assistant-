Document ID: IT-0405
Department: Information Technology
Type: Policy
Version: 2.2
Effective date: 2023-07-07
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: Remote-first Teams employees
Related documents: IT-0007, IT-0031, HR-0111

1. Purpose
This policy explains how access to systems is requested and reviewed.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Remote-first Teams employees.

3. Policy
- Access is requested through the service desk, stating the system and the business reason.
- Access to client or financial data needs the manager and the system owner to approve.
- Access unused for 90 days may be revoked and must be requested again.
- All access is reviewed every quarter.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from IT Service Desk.

5. Contact
For questions, contact it@t2.example.
