1. Purpose
This policy covers eligibility for a company car and its personal use.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Jeddah Office employees.

3. Policy
- Employees at grade M2 and above are eligible for a company car or a monthly car allowance.
- Personal use is allowed; fuel for personal use beyond 300 km a month is the employee's own cost.
- The vehicle must be serviced on schedule and any accident reported within 24 hours.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
