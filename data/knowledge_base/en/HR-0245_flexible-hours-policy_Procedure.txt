Title: Flexible Hours - Procedure (Riyadh Office)
Document ID: HR-0245
Department: Human Resources
Type: Procedure
Version: 2.2
Effective date: 2023-05-08
Status: Active
Owner: HR Operations
Applies to: Riyadh Office employees
Related documents: HR-0047, HR-0063

Purpose
This policy lets employees shift their start and end times within limits.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Start time may be chosen between 07:30 and 10:00, keeping the full daily hours.
- Any flexible arrangement must still cover the core hours.
