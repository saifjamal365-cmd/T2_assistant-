Quick reference - Promotion - Quick Reference (Jeddah Office).

Key points
- Promotions are considered 1 time(s) a year, aligned with the review cycle.
- An employee must have at least 18 months in role and a rating of "meets" or above.
- Promotions require the department head and HR to approve.
