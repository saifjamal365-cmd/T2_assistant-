Purpose
This policy explains how and when performance is reviewed.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Formal reviews take place 2 times a year, in June and December.
- Each review includes a self-assessment, manager feedback and, for senior roles, peer input.
- A rating of "needs improvement" for two cycles in a row triggers a performance plan.
