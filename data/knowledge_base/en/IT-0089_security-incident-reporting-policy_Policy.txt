Applies to: All employees
Related documents: IT-0103, LEG-0007, IT-0015

1. Purpose
This policy explains how to report a suspected security problem.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Report any suspected breach, lost device or leaked data to the security team within 2 hour(s).
- Do not try to investigate or fix it yourself; preserve evidence.
- A written summary is filed within 2 working day(s) of the report.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from IT Service Desk.

5. Contact
For questions, contact it@t2.example.
