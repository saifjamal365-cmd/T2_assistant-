Quick reference - Hot Desk Booking - Quick Reference.

Key points
- Desks in shared-seating areas are booked through the office app up to 7 days ahead.
- A desk not checked into within 15 minutes of the booking start is released to others.
- Personal items are cleared at the end of the day; the desk is shared the next day.
