Title: Bereavement Leave Policy
Document ID: HR-0025
Department: Human Resources
Type: Policy
Version: 3.1
Effective date: 2024-08-26
Status: Active
Owner: HR Operations
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: HR-0007, HR-0159

1. Purpose
This policy sets paid leave after the death of a close family member.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Employees receive 3 paid days on the death of a spouse, parent, child or sibling.
- For other relatives, 1 paid day(s) are granted.
- The manager may approve additional unpaid days on request.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
