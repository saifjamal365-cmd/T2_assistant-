1. Purpose
This policy covers out-of-hours on-call duty.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- On-call runs in 1-week rotations; the schedule is published a month ahead.
- An on-call allowance of 300 SAR per week is paid, plus overtime for call-outs.
- The on-call engineer acknowledges an alert within 15 minutes.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Operations Excellence.

5. Contact
For questions, contact ops@t2.example.
