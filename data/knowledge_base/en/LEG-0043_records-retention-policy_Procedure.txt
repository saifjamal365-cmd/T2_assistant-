Effective date: 2023-12-01
Status: Active

Applies to: All employees
Related documents: LEG-0007, IT-0063, IT-0055

Purpose
This policy states how long different records are kept.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Financial records are kept for 7 years; HR records for 7 years after an employee leaves.
- Records past their retention period are deleted in a controlled way, with a log.
- Do not delete records that are subject to a legal hold.
