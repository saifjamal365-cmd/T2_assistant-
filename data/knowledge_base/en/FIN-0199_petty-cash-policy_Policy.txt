Title: Petty Cash Policy (Cairo Office)
Document ID: FIN-0199
Department: Finance
Type: Policy
Version: 2.4
Effective date: 2023-09-01
Status: Active
Owner: Finance Shared Services
Applies to: Cairo Office employees
Related documents: FIN-0007

1. Purpose
This policy covers small cash payments held at each office.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Cairo Office employees.

3. Policy
- Petty cash is for small items only, up to 100 EGP per payment.
- Every payment needs a receipt and a signed voucher.
- The float is reconciled and topped up every month.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Finance Shared Services.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fin@t2.example.
