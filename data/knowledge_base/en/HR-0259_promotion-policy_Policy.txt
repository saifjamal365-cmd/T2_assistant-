1. Purpose
This policy describes how promotions are proposed and approved.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Riyadh Office employees.

3. Policy
- Promotions are considered 1 time(s) a year, aligned with the review cycle.
- An employee must have at least 12 months in role and a rating of "meets" or above.
- Promotions require the department head and HR to approve.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
