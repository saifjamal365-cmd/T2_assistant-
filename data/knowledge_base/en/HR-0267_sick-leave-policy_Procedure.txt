Purpose
This policy explains sick-leave entitlement and when a medical report is needed.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Employees may take up to 12 paid sick days per year.
- A medical report is required for any sick leave longer than 5 consecutive days.
- The employee must notify their manager on the first day of absence.
- Unused sick days do not carry over and are not paid out.
