Title: IT Support Service Levels (Cairo Office)
Document ID: IT-0381
Department: Information Technology
Type: Policy
Version: 2.2
Effective date: 2023-08-11
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: Cairo Office employees
Related documents: IT-0023, IT-0079

1. Purpose
This document sets response times for IT support tickets.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Cairo Office employees.

3. Policy
- Standard tickets are responded to within 1 business day(s).
- Critical issues, such as a full outage, are responded to within 1 hour(s) during business hours.
- Raise a ticket through the service desk portal, not by contacting staff directly.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from IT Service Desk.

5. Contact
For questions, contact it@t2.example.
