Quick reference - Business Travel Booking - Quick Reference.

Key points
- All travel is booked through the corporate travel portal, not directly with airlines or hotels.
- Economy class is standard; flights over 8 hours may be premium economy with manager approval.
- Hotels must not exceed the nightly cap for the destination shown in the portal.
- Book at least 7 days ahead where possible.
