Purpose
This policy sets the rules for account passwords.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Passwords must be at least 16 characters and mix upper, lower, number and symbol.
- Passwords for systems with sensitive data must be changed every 90 days.
- Never share a password. IT staff will never ask for it.
- A suspected compromise must be reported and the password changed immediately.
