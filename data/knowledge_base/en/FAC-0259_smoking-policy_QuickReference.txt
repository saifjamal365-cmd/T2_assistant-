Quick reference - Smoking - Quick Reference (Jeddah Office).

Key points
- Smoking, including e-cigarettes, is allowed only in designated outdoor areas.
- Designated areas are at least 10 metres from any building entrance.
- Smoking anywhere else on the premises, including company vehicles, is not permitted.

Owner: Facilities Management. Full policy: FAC policy set.
