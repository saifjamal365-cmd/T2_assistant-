Quick reference - Unpaid Leave - Quick Reference (Riyadh Office).

Key points
- Unpaid leave of up to 3 months may be requested after paid leave is exhausted.
- Approval requires the line manager and the department head to sign off.
- Benefits are paused during unpaid leave longer than 60 days.
