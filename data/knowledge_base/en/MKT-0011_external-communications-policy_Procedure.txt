Related documents: MKT-0007, MKT-0031, LEG-0079

Purpose
This policy covers speaking publicly on behalf of the company.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Only authorised spokespeople speak to the media or at public events on behalf of T2.
- Refer media enquiries to Corporate Communications and do not comment yourself.
- External talks and articles about company work are reviewed by Corporate Communications before release.
