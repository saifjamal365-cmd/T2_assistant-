Purpose
This policy describes how promotions are proposed and approved.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Promotions are considered 2 time(s) a year, aligned with the review cycle.
- An employee must have at least 24 months in role and a rating of "meets" or above.
- Promotions require the department head and HR to approve.
