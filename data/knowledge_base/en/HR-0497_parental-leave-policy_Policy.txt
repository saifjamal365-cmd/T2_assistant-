Applies to: Dubai Office employees
Related documents: HR-0007, HR-0015, HR-0191

1. Purpose
This policy covers paid leave for the birth or adoption of a child.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Dubai Office employees.

3. Policy
- The primary caregiver receives 90 paid days; the secondary caregiver receives 14 paid days.
- Requests should reach HR at least 30 days before the expected start date with supporting documents.
- Leave may be split into two blocks within the first year after the birth or adoption.
- Job protection applies: the employee returns to the same or an equivalent role.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
