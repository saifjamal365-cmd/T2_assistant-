Document ID: HR-0279
Department: Human Resources
Type: Procedure
Version: 2.1
Effective date: 2024-06-07
Status: Active
Owner: HR Operations
Applies to: Riyadh Office employees
Related documents: HR-0007, HR-0159

Purpose
This policy sets paid leave after the death of a close family member.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Employees receive 3 paid days on the death of a spouse, parent, child or sibling.
- For other relatives, 1 paid day(s) are granted.
- The manager may approve additional unpaid days on request.
