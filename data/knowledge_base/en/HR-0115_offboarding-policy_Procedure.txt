Title: Offboarding - Procedure
Document ID: HR-0115
Department: Human Resources
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.0
Effective date: 2023-05-19
Status: Active
Owner: HR Operations
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: HR-0127, IT-0087, IT-0023

Purpose
This policy lists what must happen when an employee leaves.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- The manager submits the leaver form at least 5 working days before the last day.
- On the last day, all equipment and access cards are returned and accounts are disabled.
- Final pay, including unused annual leave, is settled within 7 days.
