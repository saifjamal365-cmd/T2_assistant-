Purpose
This policy covers the training budget and study support.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Each employee has an annual training budget of 5000 EGP.
- Courses must be approved by the manager and be relevant to the current or next role.
- For a certification the company pays for, the employee agrees to stay 6 months or repay pro-rata.
