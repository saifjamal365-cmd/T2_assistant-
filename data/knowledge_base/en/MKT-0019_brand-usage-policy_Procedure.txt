Applies to: All employees
Related documents: MKT-0007, MKT-0015

Purpose
This policy covers correct use of the company logo and name.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Use only the current logo files from the brand portal; do not stretch, recolour or add effects.
- Partners and suppliers need written permission to use the T2 name or logo.
- Templates for slides, documents and email signatures are in the brand portal.
