Document ID: LEG-0099
Department: Legal & Compliance
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.0
Effective date: 2024-07-18
Status: Active
Owner: Legal & Compliance Office
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: HR-0127, LEG-0015, LEG-0023

Purpose
This policy explains restrictions that continue after an employee leaves.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Senior roles (grade M4 and above) may not join a direct competitor for 12 months after leaving.
- No employee may solicit T2 clients or staff for 12 months after leaving.
- The exact terms are set out in the employee's contract, not this policy alone.
