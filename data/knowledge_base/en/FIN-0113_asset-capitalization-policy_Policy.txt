Title: Asset Capitalization Policy
Document ID: FIN-0113
Department: Finance
Type: Policy
Version: 3.2
Effective date: 2023-07-24
Status: Active
Owner: Finance Shared Services
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: FIN-0039, PRC-0031

1. Purpose
This policy sets the threshold for treating a purchase as a company asset.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- A purchase of 5000 AED or more with a useful life over one year is capitalized as an asset.
- Capitalized assets are depreciated over 3 years on a straight-line basis.
- Finance tags and records every capitalized asset in the fixed asset register.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Finance Shared Services.
