Title: Lost and Found Policy
Document ID: FAC-0105
Department: Facilities
Type: Policy
Version: 3.0
Effective date: 2024-12-23
Status: Active
Owner: Facilities Management
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: FAC-0023, FAC-0031

1. Purpose
This policy covers items found on company premises.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Found items are handed to reception and logged with the date and location found.
- Unclaimed items are kept for 60 days, then donated or disposed of.
- Cash, cards and ID documents are handed to security immediately, not kept at reception.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Facilities Management.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fac@t2.example.
