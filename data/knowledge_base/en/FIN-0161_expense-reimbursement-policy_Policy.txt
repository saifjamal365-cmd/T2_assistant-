1. Purpose
This policy explains how to claim back approved business expenses.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Remote-first Teams employees.

3. Policy
- Claims are submitted through the finance portal within 45 days of the expense, with a receipt.
- Any single item over 500 EGP needs the manager's approval before it is spent.
- Alcohol, personal entertainment and traffic fines are never reimbursed.
- Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Finance Shared Services.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fin@t2.example.
