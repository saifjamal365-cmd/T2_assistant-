Title: Multi-Factor Authentication - Procedure (Dubai Office)
Document ID: IT-0295
Department: Information Technology
Type: Procedure
Version: 2.0
Effective date: 2023-11-19
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: Dubai Office employees
Related documents: IT-0007, IT-0047

Purpose
This policy requires a second factor for sign-in.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- MFA is required for email, the VPN and any system holding client or financial data.
- The approved second factor is the authenticator app; SMS is allowed only as a fallback.
- Lost devices must be reported to the service desk within 4 hours.
