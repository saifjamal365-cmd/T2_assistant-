Title: Software Licensing - Procedure
Document ID: IT-0067

Type: Procedure
Version: 3.2
Effective date: 2025-01-25
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: IT-0031, IT-0079

Purpose
This policy explains which software may be installed.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Only software approved and licensed by IT may be installed on company devices.
- Free or trial software may be used for evaluation for up to 14 days, then licensed or removed.
- Using pirated software is a serious policy violation.
