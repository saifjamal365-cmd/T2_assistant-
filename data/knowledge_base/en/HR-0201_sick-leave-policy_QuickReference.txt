Quick reference - Sick Leave - Quick Reference (Riyadh Office).

Key points
- Employees may take up to 12 paid sick days per year.
- A medical report is required for any sick leave longer than 5 consecutive days.
- The employee must notify their manager on the first day of absence.
- Unused sick days do not carry over and are not paid out.
