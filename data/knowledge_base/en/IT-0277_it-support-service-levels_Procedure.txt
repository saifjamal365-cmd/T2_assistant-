Document ID: IT-0277
Department: Information Technology
Type: Procedure
Version: 2.2
Effective date: 2025-05-10
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: Jeddah Office employees
Related documents: IT-0023, IT-0079

Purpose
This document sets response times for IT support tickets.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Standard tickets are responded to within 1 business day(s).
- Critical issues, such as a full outage, are responded to within 2 hour(s) during business hours.
- Raise a ticket through the service desk portal, not by contacting staff directly.
