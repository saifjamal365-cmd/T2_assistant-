1. Purpose
This policy covers temporary moves to another team or office.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- A secondment lasts 6 to 12 months and needs both managers to agree.
- The employee returns to their original role or an equivalent one at the end.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
