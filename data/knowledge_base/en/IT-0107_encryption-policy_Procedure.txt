Title: Encryption - Procedure
Document ID: IT-0107
Department: Information Technology
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.1
Effective date: 2024-06-25
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: IT-0063, LEG-0007

Purpose
This policy states where encryption is required.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- All company laptops and phones must have full-disk encryption enabled.
- Client or financial data sent outside the company must be encrypted or sent via the secure portal.
- Encryption keys are managed by IT and never shared by email.
