1. Purpose
This policy sets basic health and safety rules for the office.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Cairo Office employees.

3. Policy
- Any workplace injury, however minor, is reported to the office manager within 24 hours using the incident form.
- Keep walkways and fire exits clear at all times.
- Employees with symptoms of a contagious illness work from home instead of coming in.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Facilities Management.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fac@t2.example.
