Purpose
This policy covers how personal and client data must be handled.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Personal and client data is used only for the purpose it was collected for.
- Do not store client or personal data on personal devices or personal cloud accounts.
- A suspected data breach is reported to the security team within 4 hour(s).
- Access to personal data is limited to those who need it for their work.
