Applies to: Riyadh Office employees
Related documents: FAC-0007, HR-0063

Purpose
This policy covers booking a shared desk in flexible-seating areas.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Desks in shared-seating areas are booked through the office app up to 14 days ahead.
- A desk not checked into within 30 minutes of the booking start is released to others.
- Personal items are cleared at the end of the day; the desk is shared the next day.
