Title: Phishing Reporting - Procedure
Document ID: IT-0099
Department: Information Technology
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.3
Effective date: 2023-08-03
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: IT-0095, IT-0055

Purpose
This policy explains what to do with a suspicious email.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Do not click links or open attachments in a suspicious email.
- Use the "Report Phishing" button, or forward the email to phishing@t2.example.
- If you already clicked, disconnect from the network and call the service desk.

Contact
IT Service Desk: it@t2.example
