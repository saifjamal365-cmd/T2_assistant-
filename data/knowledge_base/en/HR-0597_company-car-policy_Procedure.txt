Purpose
This policy covers eligibility for a company car and its personal use.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Employees at grade M3 and above are eligible for a company car or a monthly car allowance.
- Personal use is allowed; fuel for personal use beyond 500 km a month is the employee's own cost.
- The vehicle must be serviced on schedule and any accident reported within 24 hours.
