Applies to: Jeddah Office employees

Purpose
This policy covers where smoking is allowed on company premises.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Smoking, including e-cigarettes, is allowed only in designated outdoor areas.
- Designated areas are at least 10 metres from any building entrance.
- Smoking anywhere else on the premises, including company vehicles, is not permitted.
