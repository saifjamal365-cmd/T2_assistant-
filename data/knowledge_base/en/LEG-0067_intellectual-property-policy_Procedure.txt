Title: Intellectual Property - Procedure
Document ID: LEG-0067
Department: Legal & Compliance
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.0
Effective date: 2024-12-28
Status: Active
Owner: Legal & Compliance Office
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: LEG-0015, IT-0071

Purpose
This policy states who owns work created during employment.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Work created for T2 as part of your job belongs to T2.
- Using third-party code or content requires a compatible licence, checked with Legal.
- Personal projects on your own time and equipment remain yours, if unrelated to company work.
