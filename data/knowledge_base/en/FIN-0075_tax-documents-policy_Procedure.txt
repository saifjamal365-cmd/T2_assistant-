Purpose
This policy explains how to get annual tax and salary certificates.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Salary certificates are requested through the HR system and issued within 3 working days.
- Annual tax summaries are published in the HR system by March each year.
