Purpose
This policy covers paid leave for jury duty or a court summons.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Employees summoned for jury duty or as a witness receive up to 10 paid days.
- A copy of the official summons must be given to HR as soon as it is received.
- Any fee paid by the court for attendance belongs to the employee, not the company.
