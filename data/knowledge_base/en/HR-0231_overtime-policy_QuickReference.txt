Quick reference - Overtime - Quick Reference (Riyadh Office).

Key points
- Overtime must be approved in writing by the manager before it is worked.
- Overtime is paid at 1.25 times the normal hourly rate.
- Employees at grade M4 and above are not eligible for overtime pay.
