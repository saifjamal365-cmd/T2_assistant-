Version: 3.0
Effective date: 2024-01-16
Status: Active

Applies to: All employees
Related documents: IT-0079, FAC-0039, HR-0063

1. Purpose
This policy helps employees set up a healthy workspace.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- A workstation assessment is offered to every new employee and on request.
- The company provides an adjustable chair and, on assessment, extras such as a footrest or riser.
- Remote employees can claim up to 1500 AED toward a home-office chair or desk.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Facilities Management.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fac@t2.example.
