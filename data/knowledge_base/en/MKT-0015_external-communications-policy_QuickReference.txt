Quick reference - External Communications - Quick Reference.

Key points
- Only authorised spokespeople speak to the media or at public events on behalf of T2.
- Refer media enquiries to Corporate Communications and do not comment yourself.
- External talks and articles about company work are reviewed by Corporate Communications before release.
