Purpose
This policy explains how an unresolved client issue is escalated.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- If the first point of contact cannot resolve an issue within 48 hours, it is escalated to the account manager.
- Issues with possible financial loss or a public complaint are flagged to the department head within 2 hours.
- Every escalation is logged in the CRM with a timeline of actions.
