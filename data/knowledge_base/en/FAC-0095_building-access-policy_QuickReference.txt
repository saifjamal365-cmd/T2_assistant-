Quick reference - Building Access - Quick Reference (Riyadh Office).

Key points
- Every employee has an access card. Do not let anyone "tailgate" in behind you.
- Lost cards are reported the same day; a replacement costs 100 AED.
- After-hours access is logged and may need manager approval.
