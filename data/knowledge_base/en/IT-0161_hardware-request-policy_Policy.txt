Applies to: Riyadh Office employees
Related documents: IT-0087, IT-0071, FAC-0063

1. Purpose
This policy explains how to request laptops, monitors and phones.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Riyadh Office employees.

3. Policy
- Standard equipment is requested through the asset portal with a business justification.
- Standard requests are approved by the manager and fulfilled within 5 working days.
- Non-standard items also need IT approval and may take longer.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from IT Service Desk.

5. Contact
For questions, contact it@t2.example.
