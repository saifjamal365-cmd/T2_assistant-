Title: Clear Desk and Screen - Procedure (Riyadh Office)
Document ID: IT-0171
Department: Information Technology
Type: Procedure
Version: 2.3
Effective date: 2025-03-10
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: Riyadh Office employees
Related documents: IT-0127, LEG-0007

Purpose
This policy protects information left on desks and screens.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Lock your screen whenever you leave your desk.
- Documents with client or personal data are locked away at the end of the day.
- Printouts left on the printer for more than 15 minutes are shredded.
