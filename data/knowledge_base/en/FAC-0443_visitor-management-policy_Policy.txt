Effective date: 2024-01-26

Applies to: Remote-first Teams employees
Related documents: FAC-0023, LEG-0007

1. Purpose
This policy covers hosting visitors in the office.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Remote-first Teams employees.

3. Policy
- Visitors are registered in advance and are met and escorted by their host.
- Visitors sign in at reception, wear a badge, and sign out when leaving.
- Visitors are not left alone in areas with client or personal data.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Facilities Management.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fac@t2.example.
