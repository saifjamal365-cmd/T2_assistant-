Purpose
This policy covers items found on company premises.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Found items are handed to reception and logged with the date and location found.
- Unclaimed items are kept for 90 days, then donated or disposed of.
- Cash, cards and ID documents are handed to security immediately, not kept at reception.
