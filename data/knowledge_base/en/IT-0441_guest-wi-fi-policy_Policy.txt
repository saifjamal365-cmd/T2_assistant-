Title: Guest Wi-Fi Policy (Remote-first Teams)
Document ID: IT-0441
Department: Information Technology
Type: Policy
Version: 2.4
Effective date: 2023-10-01
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: Remote-first Teams employees
Related documents: FAC-0031, IT-0047

1. Purpose
This policy covers internet access for visitors.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Remote-first Teams employees.

3. Policy
- Guests are given access to a separate guest network only; it cannot reach internal systems.
- The guest Wi-Fi password is changed every 30 days and is available from reception.
- A staff member sponsoring a guest is responsible for that guest's network use.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from IT Service Desk.

5. Contact
For questions, contact it@t2.example.
