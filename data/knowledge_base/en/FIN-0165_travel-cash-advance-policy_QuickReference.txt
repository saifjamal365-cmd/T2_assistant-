Quick reference - Travel Cash Advance - Quick Reference (Jeddah Office).

Key points
- An advance of up to 3000 may be requested for trip costs not covered by the corporate card.
- The advance is requested at least 3 days before travel through the finance portal.
- The advance is reconciled against receipts within 14 days of returning; any unused amount is returned.
