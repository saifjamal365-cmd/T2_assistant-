Quick reference - Procurement - Quick Reference.

Key points
- A purchase request is raised before any commitment to a supplier.
- Purchases up to 5000 AED need one quote; above 5000 need 2 quotes.
- Purchases over 50000 AED go to the Procurement Committee.
