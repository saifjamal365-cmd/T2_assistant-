Quick reference - Procurement - Quick Reference.

Key points
- A purchase request is raised before any commitment to a supplier.
- Purchases up to 10000 AED need one quote; above 10000 need 2 quotes.
- Purchases over 100000 AED go to the Procurement Committee.
