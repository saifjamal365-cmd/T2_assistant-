Applies to: Cairo Office employees
Related documents: IT-0087, IT-0071, FAC-0063

Purpose
This policy explains how to request laptops, monitors and phones.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Standard equipment is requested through the asset portal with a business justification.
- Standard requests are approved by the manager and fulfilled within 3 working days.
- Non-standard items also need IT approval and may take longer.
