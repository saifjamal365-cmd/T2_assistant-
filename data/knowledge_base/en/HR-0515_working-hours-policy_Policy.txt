1. Purpose
This policy defines standard working hours and core hours.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Dubai Office employees.

3. Policy
- The standard work week is 45 hours over 5 days.
- Core hours, when everyone must be reachable, are 10:00 to 16:00.
- Time is recorded in the HR system; managers review timesheets weekly.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
