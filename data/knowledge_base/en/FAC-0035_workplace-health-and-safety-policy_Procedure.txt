Status: Active

Applies to: All employees
Related documents: FAC-0047, FAC-0055, FAC-0063

Purpose
This policy sets basic health and safety rules for the office.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Any workplace injury, however minor, is reported to the office manager within 48 hours using the incident form.
- Keep walkways and fire exits clear at all times.
- Employees with symptoms of a contagious illness work from home instead of coming in.
