1. Purpose
This policy states the response and resolution targets for client-facing services.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- First response to a client request is within 4 business hours.
- Standard requests are resolved within 5 business days.
- Service levels are reviewed with each client every quarter.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Operations Excellence.

5. Contact
For questions, contact ops@t2.example.
