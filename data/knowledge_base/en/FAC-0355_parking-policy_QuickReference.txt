Quick reference - Parking - Quick Reference (Cairo Office).

Key points
- Parking is limited. Spaces are assigned by the waiting list, reviewed every 3 months.
- Reserved spaces are for visitors and accessibility needs only.
- Parking in a reserved space without a permit may lead to the car being clamped.

Owner: Facilities Management. Full policy: FAC policy set.
