Quick reference - Payroll Schedule - Quick Reference.

Key points
- Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month, or the previous working day if that is a weekend or holiday.
- Changes to bank details must reach Payroll 3 working days before payday.
- Payslips are available in the HR system on payday.
