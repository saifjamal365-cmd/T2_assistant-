Title: Generative AI Usage - Procedure
Document ID: IT-0147
Department: Information Technology
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.3
Effective date: 2024-10-28
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: LEG-0007, IT-0031, LEG-0015

Purpose
This policy covers using AI chat and coding tools for work.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Only AI tools on the IT-approved list may be used; a new tool needs IT review before use.
- Client data, personal data and unreleased company information must never be entered into a public AI tool.
- AI-generated content used externally must be reviewed by a person before it is sent or published.
