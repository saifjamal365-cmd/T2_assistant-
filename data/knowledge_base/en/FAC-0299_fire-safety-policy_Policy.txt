1. Purpose
This policy covers fire drills and evacuation.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Dubai Office employees.

3. Policy
- Fire drills are held 3 times a year and attendance is mandatory for everyone on site.
- On the alarm, leave by the nearest exit and go to the assembly point. Do not use lifts.
- Fire wardens check their area and report headcount at the assembly point.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Facilities Management.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fac@t2.example.
