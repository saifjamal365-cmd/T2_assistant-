Applies to: Cairo Office employees
Related documents: HR-0103, FIN-0079

1. Purpose
This document states when salaries are paid.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Cairo Office employees.

3. Policy
- Salaries are paid on the last day of each month, or the previous working day if that is a weekend or holiday.
- Changes to bank details must reach Payroll 3 working days before payday.
- Payslips are available in the HR system on payday.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Finance Shared Services.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fin@t2.example.
