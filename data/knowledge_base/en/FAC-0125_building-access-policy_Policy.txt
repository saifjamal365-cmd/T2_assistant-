Applies to: Riyadh Office employees
Related documents: FAC-0015, FAC-0031

1. Purpose
This policy covers access cards and entry to the office.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Riyadh Office employees.

3. Policy
- Every employee has an access card. Do not let anyone "tailgate" in behind you.
- Lost cards are reported the same day; a replacement costs 50 AED.
- After-hours access is logged and may need manager approval.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Facilities Management.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fac@t2.example.
