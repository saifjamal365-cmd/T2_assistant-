Applies to: Remote-first Teams employees
Related documents: FAC-0031, FAC-0015

1. Purpose
This policy explains how to reserve and use meeting rooms.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Remote-first Teams employees.

3. Policy
- Rooms are booked through the shared calendar. Walk-in use is allowed only if the room is free for the next 30 minutes.
- Bookings longer than 3 hours need an office administrator to approve.
- If a meeting ends early or is cancelled, release the room in the calendar.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Facilities Management.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fac@t2.example.
