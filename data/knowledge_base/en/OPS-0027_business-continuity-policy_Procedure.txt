Purpose
This policy covers keeping key services running during a disruption.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Each team has a continuity plan naming its critical services and recovery time objective.
- Continuity plans are tested at least once a year.
- During a major disruption, the crisis team leads and communicates every 2 hour(s).
