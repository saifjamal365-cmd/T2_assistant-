Title: Competition Law - Procedure

Department: Legal & Compliance
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.2
Effective date: 2024-05-07
Status: Active
Owner: Legal & Compliance Office
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: LEG-0015, MKT-0015

Purpose
This policy helps employees avoid anti-competitive behaviour.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Do not discuss prices, customers or markets with competitors.
- Leave any meeting where competitors start such a discussion, and tell Legal.
- Market information about competitors is gathered only from public sources.
