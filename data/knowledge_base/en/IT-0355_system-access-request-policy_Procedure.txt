Purpose
This policy explains how access to systems is requested and reviewed.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Access is requested through the service desk, stating the system and the business reason.
- Access to client or financial data needs the manager and the system owner to approve.
- Access unused for 60 days may be revoked and must be requested again.
- All access is reviewed every quarter.
