Applies to: All employees
Related documents: IT-0111, LEG-0047

1. Purpose
This policy explains what is backed up and how often.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Files on approved company storage are backed up automatically every night.
- Backups are kept for 30 days and are tested every quarter.
- Files kept only on a local laptop are not backed up and may be lost.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from IT Service Desk.

5. Contact
For questions, contact it@t2.example.
