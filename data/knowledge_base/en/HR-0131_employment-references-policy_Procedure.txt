Title: Employment References - Procedure
Document ID: HR-0131
Department: Human Resources
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.2
Effective date: 2025-02-06
Status: Active
Owner: HR Operations
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: HR-0119, LEG-0015

Purpose
This policy states how the company responds to reference requests.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Only HR may issue an official reference; it confirms title and dates of employment only.
- Personal references from managers must state that they are personal, not on behalf of T2.
