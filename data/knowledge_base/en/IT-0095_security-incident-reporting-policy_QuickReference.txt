Quick reference - Security Incident Reporting - Quick Reference.

Key points
- Report any suspected breach, lost device or leaked data to the security team within 2 hour(s).
- Do not try to investigate or fix it yourself; preserve evidence.
- A written summary is filed within 1 working day(s) of the report.
