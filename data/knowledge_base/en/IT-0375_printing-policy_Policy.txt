Applies to: Cairo Office employees

1. Purpose
This policy covers secure and economical printing.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Cairo Office employees.

3. Policy
- Printing needs a badge tap at the printer to release the job.
- Default printing is double-sided and black-and-white.
- Unreleased jobs are deleted after 2 hours.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from IT Service Desk.

5. Contact
For questions, contact it@t2.example.
