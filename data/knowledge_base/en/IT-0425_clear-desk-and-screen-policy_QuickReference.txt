Quick reference - Clear Desk and Screen - Quick Reference (Remote-first Teams).

Key points
- Lock your screen whenever you leave your desk.
- Documents with client or personal data are locked away at the end of the day.
- Printouts left on the printer for more than 60 minutes are shredded.
