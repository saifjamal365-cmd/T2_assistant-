1. Purpose
This policy covers talking about work on personal social media.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- You may discuss your work on personal accounts, but not confidential or unreleased information.
- Make clear that opinions are your own, not an official position of T2.
- Only the Corporate Communications team posts on official T2 accounts.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Corporate Communications.

5. Contact
For questions, contact mkt@t2.example.
