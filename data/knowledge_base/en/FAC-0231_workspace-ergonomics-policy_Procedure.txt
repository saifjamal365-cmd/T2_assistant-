Purpose
This policy helps employees set up a healthy workspace.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- A workstation assessment is offered to every new employee and on request.
- The company provides an adjustable chair and, on assessment, extras such as a footrest or riser.
- Remote employees can claim up to 500 EGP toward a home-office chair or desk.
