Quick reference - Supplier Selection - Quick Reference.

Key points
- Selection is based on written criteria: price, quality, delivery, and risk.
- For spend over 25000 SAR, at least 3 suppliers are invited to quote.
- A conflict of interest with a supplier is declared and the person steps out of the decision.

Owner: Procurement Office. Full policy: PRC policy set.
