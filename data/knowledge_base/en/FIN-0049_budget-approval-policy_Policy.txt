Applies to: All employees
Related documents: FIN-0039, PRC-0031

1. Purpose
This policy sets who can approve spending against a budget.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Managers may approve spend within their budget up to 10000 EGP per item.
- Department heads approve up to 50000 EGP; anything higher needs the CFO.
- Spend not in the approved budget needs a business case.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Finance Shared Services.

5. Contact
