Purpose
This policy explains how changes to live systems are approved.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Every change to a live system is recorded with a plan, a test and a rollback step.
- Standard low-risk changes are pre-approved; other changes go to the change board.
- Changes are not deployed on Thursday or during a change freeze.
