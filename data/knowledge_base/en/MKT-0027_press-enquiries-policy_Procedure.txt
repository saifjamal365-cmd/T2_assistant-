Type: Procedure
Version: 3.2
Effective date: 2023-08-12
Status: Active
Owner: Corporate Communications
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: MKT-0015

Purpose
This policy explains what to do when a journalist makes contact.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Do not confirm, deny or comment on anything, even off the record.
- Take the journalist's name, outlet, question and deadline, and pass them to Corporate Communications the same day.
- Corporate Communications coordinates a single approved response.
