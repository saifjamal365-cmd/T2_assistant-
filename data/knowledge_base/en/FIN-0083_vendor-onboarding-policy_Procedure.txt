Purpose
This policy explains how a new supplier is set up for payment.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- A new vendor provides a trade licence, bank letter and tax registration before setup.
- Bank details are verified by a call-back to a number on the trade licence, not the invoice.
- Vendor records are reviewed every 24 months.
