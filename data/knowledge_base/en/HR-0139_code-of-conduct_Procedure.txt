Related documents: HR-0151, LEG-0023, LEG-0063

Purpose
The code sets the standard of behaviour expected from every employee.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Treat colleagues and clients with respect; discrimination and harassment are not tolerated.
- Any conflict of interest must be disclosed in writing to HR Operations.
- Breaching the code may lead to disciplinary action up to termination.
