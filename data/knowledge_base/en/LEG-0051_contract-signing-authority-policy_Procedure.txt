Title: Contract Signing Authority - Procedure
Document ID: LEG-0051
Department: Legal & Compliance
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.1
Effective date: 2023-05-23
Status: Active
Owner: Legal & Compliance Office
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: FIN-0039, FIN-0055

Purpose
This policy states who is allowed to sign contracts on behalf of the company.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Only named signatories in the authority matrix may sign contracts for T2.
- Contracts over 1000000 EGP or longer than 5 years need board approval.
- Legal reviews every contract before signing.
