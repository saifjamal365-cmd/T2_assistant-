Applies to: All employees
Related documents: IT-0111, LEG-0047

Purpose
This policy explains what is backed up and how often.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Files on approved company storage are backed up automatically every 4 hours.
- Backups are kept for 90 days and are tested every quarter.
- Files kept only on a local laptop are not backed up and may be lost.
