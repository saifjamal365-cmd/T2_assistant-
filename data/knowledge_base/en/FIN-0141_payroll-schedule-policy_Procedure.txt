Document ID: FIN-0141

Type: Procedure
Version: 2.0
Effective date: 2023-05-12
Status: Active
Owner: Finance Shared Services
Applies to: Dubai Office employees
Related documents: HR-0103, FIN-0079

Purpose
This document states when salaries are paid.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month, or the previous working day if that is a weekend or holiday.
- Changes to bank details must reach Payroll 5 working days before payday.
- Payslips are available in the HR system on payday.
