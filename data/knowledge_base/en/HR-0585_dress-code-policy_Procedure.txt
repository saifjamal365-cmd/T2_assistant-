Purpose
This policy describes what to wear in the office and with clients.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Standard office wear is smart-casual.
- Client meetings and external events require business attire.
- Thursday is a casual day, except where a client is on site.
