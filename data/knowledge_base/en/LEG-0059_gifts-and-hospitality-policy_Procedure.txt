Title: Gifts and Hospitality - Procedure
Document ID: LEG-0059
Department: Legal & Compliance
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.3
Effective date: 2025-01-02
Status: Active
Owner: Legal & Compliance Office
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: LEG-0023, LEG-0031

Purpose
This policy sets limits on giving and receiving gifts and hospitality.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Gifts or hospitality worth more than 300 SAR must be declared in the register.
- Never accept cash or a cash equivalent.
- Do not give or accept anything around the time of a tender or contract decision.
