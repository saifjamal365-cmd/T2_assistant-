Purpose
This policy covers logging and responding to customer feedback.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- All customer feedback, positive or negative, is logged in the CRM within 24 hours of receipt.
- A negative comment about safety or fraud is escalated to the department head immediately.
- The customer receives an acknowledgement within one business day, even if the fix takes longer.
