Title: Event and Sponsorship Approval Policy

Type: Policy
Version: 3.2
Effective date: 2024-10-07
Status: Active
Owner: Corporate Communications
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: FIN-0055, MKT-0023

1. Purpose
This policy covers approving company events and sponsorships.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Any event or sponsorship using the T2 name needs Corporate Communications approval before commitments are made.
- Requests are submitted at least 4 weeks ahead with a budget and objective.
- Sponsorships over 50000 SAR also need Finance approval.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Corporate Communications.

5. Contact
For questions, contact mkt@t2.example.
