Purpose
This policy states the response and resolution targets for client-facing services.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- First response to a client request is within 8 business hours.
- Standard requests are resolved within 5 business days.
- Service levels are reviewed with each client every quarter.
