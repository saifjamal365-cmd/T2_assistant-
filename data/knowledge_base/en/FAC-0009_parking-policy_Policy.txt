1. Purpose
This policy explains how office parking is allocated.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Parking is limited. Spaces are assigned by the waiting list, reviewed every 12 months.
- Reserved spaces are for visitors and accessibility needs only.
- Parking in a reserved space without a permit may lead to the car being clamped.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Facilities Management.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fac@t2.example.
