Version: 3.1
Effective date: 2023-08-25
Status: Active
Owner: Procurement Office
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: FIN-0039, FIN-0047

Purpose
This policy makes sure supplier contracts are reviewed before they renew.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Procurement reviews every contract 2 months before its renewal date.
- Auto-renewal clauses are avoided; renewals are an active decision.
- The review checks price, usage, service quality and whether the need still exists.
