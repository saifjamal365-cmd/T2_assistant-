Quick reference - Remote Work - Quick Reference (Riyadh Office).

Key points
- Approved employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
- Remote employees must join scheduled meetings with the camera on unless agreed otherwise.
- Company equipment used remotely must connect only through the approved VPN.
- A fully remote arrangement needs HR approval and is reviewed every 6 months.
