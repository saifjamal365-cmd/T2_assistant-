1. Purpose
This policy explains when overtime is paid and how it is approved.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Overtime must be approved in writing by the manager before it is worked.
- Overtime is paid at 2 times the normal hourly rate.
- Employees at grade M3 and above are not eligible for overtime pay.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
