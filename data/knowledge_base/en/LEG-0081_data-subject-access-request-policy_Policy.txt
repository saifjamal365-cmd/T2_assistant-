Applies to: All employees
Related documents: LEG-0007, LEG-0015, LEG-0047

1. Purpose
This policy covers a request from a person to see or delete their personal data.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- A data subject request is forwarded to Legal & Compliance the same day it is received.
- The company responds within 45 days, as required by data protection law.
- The requester's identity is verified before any personal data is shared or deleted.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Legal & Compliance Office.

5. Contact
For questions, contact leg@t2.example.
