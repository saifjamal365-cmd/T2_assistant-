Document ID: HR-0099

Type: Procedure
Version: 3.1
Effective date: 2023-03-10
Status: Active
Owner: HR Operations
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: HR-0087, HR-0095

Purpose
This policy explains the annual salary review.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Salaries are reviewed once a year, effective 1 April.
- Adjustments depend on the performance rating, the salary band and the annual budget.
- Out-of-cycle adjustments need Compensation Committee approval.
