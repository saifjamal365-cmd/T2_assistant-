1. Purpose
This policy explains what to do when a journalist makes contact.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Do not confirm, deny or comment on anything, even off the record.
- Take the journalist's name, outlet, question and deadline, and pass them to Corporate Communications the same day.
- Corporate Communications coordinates a single approved response.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Corporate Communications.

5. Contact
For questions, contact mkt@t2.example.
