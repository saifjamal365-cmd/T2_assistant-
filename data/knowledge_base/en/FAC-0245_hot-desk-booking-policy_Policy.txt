Department: Facilities

Version: 2.0
Effective date: 2023-07-03
Status: Active
Owner: Facilities Management
Applies to: Jeddah Office employees
Related documents: FAC-0007, HR-0063

1. Purpose
This policy covers booking a shared desk in flexible-seating areas.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Jeddah Office employees.

3. Policy
- Desks in shared-seating areas are booked through the office app up to 5 days ahead.
- A desk not checked into within 15 minutes of the booking start is released to others.
- Personal items are cleared at the end of the day; the desk is shared the next day.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Facilities Management.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fac@t2.example.
