Quick reference - Clear Desk and Screen - Quick Reference (Riyadh Office).

Key points
- Lock your screen whenever you leave your desk.
- Documents with client or personal data are locked away at the end of the day.
- Printouts left on the printer for more than 15 minutes are shredded.
