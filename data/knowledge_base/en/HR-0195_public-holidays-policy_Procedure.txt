Purpose
This policy lists paid public holidays and what happens if one falls on a weekend.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- T2 observes 13 paid public holidays a year, published on the HR calendar each January.
- If a holiday falls on a weekend, a floating day is given instead, to be used within 3 months.
- Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a replacement day plus their normal pay.
