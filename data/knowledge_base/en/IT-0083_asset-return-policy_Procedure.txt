Version: 3.2
Effective date: 2025-05-21
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: HR-0119, IT-0079

Purpose
This policy covers returning company equipment.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- All company equipment remains company property and is returned on the last working day.
- Damaged or missing items beyond normal wear may be charged to the employee.
- Equipment for a role change is swapped through the service desk.
