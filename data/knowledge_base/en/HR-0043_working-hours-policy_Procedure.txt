Purpose
This policy defines standard working hours and core hours.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- The standard work week is 42 hours over 5 days.
- Core hours, when everyone must be reachable, are 10:00 to 16:00.
- Time is recorded in the HR system; managers review timesheets weekly.
