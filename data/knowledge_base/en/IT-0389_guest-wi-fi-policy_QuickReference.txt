Quick reference - Guest Wi-Fi - Quick Reference (Cairo Office).

Key points
- Guests are given access to a separate guest network only; it cannot reach internal systems.
- The guest Wi-Fi password is changed every 7 days and is available from reception.
- A staff member sponsoring a guest is responsible for that guest's network use.

Owner: IT Service Desk. Full policy: IT policy set.
