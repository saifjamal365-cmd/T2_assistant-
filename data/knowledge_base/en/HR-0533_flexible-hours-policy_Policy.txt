Title: Flexible Hours Policy (Dubai Office)
Document ID: HR-0533
Department: Human Resources
Type: Policy
Version: 2.2
Effective date: 2024-09-28
Status: Active
Owner: HR Operations
Applies to: Dubai Office employees
Related documents: HR-0047, HR-0063

1. Purpose
This policy lets employees shift their start and end times within limits.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Dubai Office employees.

3. Policy
- Start time may be chosen between 07:00 and 09:30, keeping the full daily hours.
- Any flexible arrangement must still cover the core hours.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
