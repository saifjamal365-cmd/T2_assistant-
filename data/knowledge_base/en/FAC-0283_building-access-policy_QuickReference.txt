Quick reference - Building Access - Quick Reference (Dubai Office).

Key points
- Every employee has an access card. Do not let anyone "tailgate" in behind you.
- Lost cards are reported the same day; a replacement costs 50 EGP.
- After-hours access is logged and may need manager approval.
