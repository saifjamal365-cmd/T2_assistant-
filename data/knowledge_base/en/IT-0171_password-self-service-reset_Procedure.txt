Title: Password Self-Service Reset - Procedure
Document ID: IT-0171
Department: Information Technology
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.4
Effective date: 2025-05-16
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: IT-0007, IT-0135

Purpose
This policy covers resetting a forgotten password without contacting the help desk.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Employees can reset a forgotten password through the self-service portal after verifying identity.
- The account locks after 3 failed sign-in attempts and unlocks after 15 minutes.
- If self-service verification fails, contact the service desk with a photo ID.
