Purpose
This policy explains how to claim back approved business expenses.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Claims are submitted through the finance portal within 45 days of the expense, with a receipt.
- Any single item over 1000 SAR needs the manager's approval before it is spent.
- Alcohol, personal entertainment and traffic fines are never reimbursed.
- Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run.
