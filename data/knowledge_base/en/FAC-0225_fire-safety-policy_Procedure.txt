Purpose
This policy covers fire drills and evacuation.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Fire drills are held 2 times a year and attendance is mandatory for everyone on site.
- On the alarm, leave by the nearest exit and go to the assembly point. Do not use lifts.
- Fire wardens check their area and report headcount at the assembly point.
