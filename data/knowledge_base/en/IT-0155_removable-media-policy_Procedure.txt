Effective date: 2023-02-08
Status: Active

Applies to: All employees
Related documents: IT-0063, IT-0111, IT-0095

Purpose
This policy covers using USB drives and other removable storage.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Company data may only be copied to removable media that is encrypted and IT-issued.
- Personal USB drives must not be connected to a company device.
- Lost removable media containing company data is reported to IT within 4 hours.
