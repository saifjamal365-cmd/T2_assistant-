Purpose
This policy explains how supplier invoices are approved and paid.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- An invoice is paid only against an approved purchase order and a goods-received note.
- Standard payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date.
- Invoices without a purchase order are returned to the supplier.
