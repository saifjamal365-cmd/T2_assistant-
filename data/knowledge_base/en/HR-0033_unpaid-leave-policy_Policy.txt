Related documents: HR-0007, HR-0015

1. Purpose
This policy explains when employees can take unpaid leave.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Unpaid leave of up to 6 months may be requested after paid leave is exhausted.
- Approval requires the line manager and the department head to sign off.
- Benefits are paused during unpaid leave longer than 60 days.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
