Quick reference - Expense Reimbursement - Quick Reference (Riyadh Office).

Key points
- Claims are submitted through the finance portal within 14 days of the expense, with a receipt.
- Any single item over 500 SAR needs the manager's approval before it is spent.
- Alcohol, personal entertainment and traffic fines are never reimbursed.
- Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run.
