Title: Purchase Approval Thresholds
Document ID: PRC-0025
Department: Procurement
Type: Policy
Version: 3.1
Effective date: 2023-09-08
Status: Active
Owner: Procurement Office
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: FIN-0055, FIN-0039, PRC-0007

1. Purpose
This quick reference lists the approval level needed for each spend amount.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Up to 10000 AED: line manager.
- 10000 to 50000 AED: department head.
- Over 50000 AED: Procurement Committee and CFO.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Procurement Office.

5. Contact
For questions, contact prc@t2.example.
