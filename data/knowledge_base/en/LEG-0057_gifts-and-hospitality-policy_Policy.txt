Title: Gifts and Hospitality Policy

Version: 3.2
Effective date: 2023-11-10
Status: Active
Owner: Legal & Compliance Office
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: LEG-0023, LEG-0031

1. Purpose
This policy sets limits on giving and receiving gifts and hospitality.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Gifts or hospitality worth more than 200 AED must be declared in the register.
- Never accept cash or a cash equivalent.
- Do not give or accept anything around the time of a tender or contract decision.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Legal & Compliance Office.

5. Contact
For questions, contact leg@t2.example.
