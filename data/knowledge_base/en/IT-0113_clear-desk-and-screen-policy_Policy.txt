Title: Clear Desk and Screen Policy
Document ID: IT-0113
Department: Information Technology
Type: Policy
Version: 3.4
Effective date: 2024-01-01
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: IT-0127, LEG-0007

1. Purpose
This policy protects information left on desks and screens.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Lock your screen whenever you leave your desk.
- Documents with client or personal data are locked away at the end of the day.
- Printouts left on the printer for more than 30 minutes are shredded.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from IT Service Desk.

5. Contact
For questions, contact it@t2.example.
