Applies to: All employees
Related documents: HR-0183, HR-0023

Purpose
This policy explains support when an employee moves offices or cities for work.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- The company covers shipping up to 300 kg and 30 nights of temporary accommodation.
- A relocation allowance of 10000 SAR is paid once, on arrival.
- If the employee resigns within 24 months, the allowance is repaid pro-rata.
