1. Purpose
This policy covers the company credit card.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- The card is for business expenses only; personal use is not allowed.
- Receipts and a short note are uploaded within 5 days of each transaction.
- Undocumented transactions after 30 days may be deducted from salary.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Finance Shared Services.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fin@t2.example.
