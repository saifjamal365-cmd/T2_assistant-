Quick reference - Bereavement Leave - Quick Reference (Jeddah Office).

Key points
- Employees receive 5 paid days on the death of a spouse, parent, child or sibling.
- For other relatives, 1 paid day(s) are granted.
- The manager may approve additional unpaid days on request.
