Department: Facilities
Type: Policy
Version: 2.3
Effective date: 2023-04-02
Status: Active
Owner: Facilities Management
Applies to: Dubai Office employees

1. Purpose
This policy covers incoming and outgoing post and parcels.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Dubai Office employees.

3. Policy
- Personal parcels should not be sent to the office address.
- Outgoing business courier is booked through reception with a cost code.
- Reception holds parcels for 10 days before returning them to sender.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Facilities Management.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fac@t2.example.
