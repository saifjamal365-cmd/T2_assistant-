Version: 3.1

Applies to: All employees
Related documents: FAC-0015, FAC-0023

1. Purpose
This policy covers using electric-vehicle charging points at the office.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- EV charging bays are booked through the office app; a session is limited to 3 hours.
- Charging is free for employees; moving the car promptly once charged is expected.
- Non-EVs must not park in a charging bay, even briefly.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Facilities Management.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fac@t2.example.
