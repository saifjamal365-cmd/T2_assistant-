Effective date: 2024-01-14

Applies to: Cairo Office employees
Related documents: FAC-0015, FAC-0031

Purpose
This policy covers access cards and entry to the office.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Every employee has an access card. Do not let anyone "tailgate" in behind you.
- Lost cards are reported the same day; a replacement costs 50 SAR.
- After-hours access is logged and may need manager approval.
