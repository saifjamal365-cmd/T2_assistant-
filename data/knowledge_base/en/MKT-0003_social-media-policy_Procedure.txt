Applies to: All employees
Related documents: MKT-0015, LEG-0015, MKT-0023

Purpose
This policy covers talking about work on personal social media.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- You may discuss your work on personal accounts, but not confidential or unreleased information.
- Make clear that opinions are your own, not an official position of T2.
- Only the Corporate Communications team posts on official T2 accounts.
