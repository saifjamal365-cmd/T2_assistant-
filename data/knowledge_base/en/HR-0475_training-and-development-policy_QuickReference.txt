Quick reference - Training and Development - Quick Reference (Jeddah Office).

Key points
- Each employee has an annual training budget of 3000 EGP.
- Courses must be approved by the manager and be relevant to the current or next role.
- For a certification the company pays for, the employee agrees to stay 6 months or repay pro-rata.
