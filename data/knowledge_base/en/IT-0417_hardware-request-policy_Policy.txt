Title: Hardware Request Policy (Remote-first Teams)
Document ID: IT-0417
Department: Information Technology
Type: Policy
Version: 2.1
Effective date: 2023-04-29
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: Remote-first Teams employees
Related documents: IT-0087, IT-0071, FAC-0063

1. Purpose
This policy explains how to request laptops, monitors and phones.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Remote-first Teams employees.

3. Policy
- Standard equipment is requested through the asset portal with a business justification.
- Standard requests are approved by the manager and fulfilled within 10 working days.
- Non-standard items also need IT approval and may take longer.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from IT Service Desk.

5. Contact
For questions, contact it@t2.example.
