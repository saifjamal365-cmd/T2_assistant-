Quick reference - Meeting Room Booking - Quick Reference (Jeddah Office).

Key points
- Rooms are booked through the shared calendar. Walk-in use is allowed only if the room is free for the next 15 minutes.
- Bookings longer than 2 hours need an office administrator to approve.
- If a meeting ends early or is cancelled, release the room in the calendar.

Owner: Facilities Management. Full policy: FAC policy set.
