Quick reference - Workplace Health and Safety - Quick Reference (Remote-first Teams).

Key points
- Any workplace injury, however minor, is reported to the office manager within 48 hours using the incident form.
- Keep walkways and fire exits clear at all times.
- Employees with symptoms of a contagious illness work from home instead of coming in.
