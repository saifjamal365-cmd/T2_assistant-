Purpose
This policy explains how office parking is allocated.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Parking is limited. Spaces are assigned by the waiting list, reviewed every 3 months.
- Reserved spaces are for visitors and accessibility needs only.
- Parking in a reserved space without a permit may lead to the car being clamped.
