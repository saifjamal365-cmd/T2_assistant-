Department: Facilities

Version: 2.3
Effective date: 2024-04-03
Status: Active
Owner: Facilities Management
Applies to: Dubai Office employees
Related documents: FAC-0007, HR-0063

1. Purpose
This policy covers booking a shared desk in flexible-seating areas.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Dubai Office employees.

3. Policy
- Desks in shared-seating areas are booked through the office app up to 7 days ahead.
- A desk not checked into within 30 minutes of the booking start is released to others.
- Personal items are cleared at the end of the day; the desk is shared the next day.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Facilities Management.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fac@t2.example.
