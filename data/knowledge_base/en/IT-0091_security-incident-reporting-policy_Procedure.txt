Title: Security Incident Reporting - Procedure
Document ID: IT-0091
Department: Information Technology
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.2
Effective date: 2023-02-17
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: IT-0103, LEG-0007, IT-0015

Purpose
This policy explains how to report a suspected security problem.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Report any suspected breach, lost device or leaked data to the security team within 1 hour(s).
- Do not try to investigate or fix it yourself; preserve evidence.
- A written summary is filed within 1 working day(s) of the report.
