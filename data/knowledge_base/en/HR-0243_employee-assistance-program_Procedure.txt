Purpose
This policy covers free, confidential counselling for employees.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Employees and their immediate family may access up to 6 free counselling sessions a year.
- Sessions are confidential; HR and managers are not told who used the service.
- The service is reached through the EAP hotline, available 24/7.
