Title: Flexible Hours Policy
Document ID: HR-0065
Department: Human Resources
Type: Policy
Version: 3.2
Effective date: 2024-06-30
Status: Active
Owner: HR Operations
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: HR-0047, HR-0063

1. Purpose
This policy lets employees shift their start and end times within limits.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Start time may be chosen between 07:00 and 10:00, keeping the full daily hours.
- Any flexible arrangement must still cover the core hours.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
