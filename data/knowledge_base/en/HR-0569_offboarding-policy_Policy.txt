Applies to: Dubai Office employees
Related documents: HR-0127, IT-0087, IT-0023

1. Purpose
This policy lists what must happen when an employee leaves.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Dubai Office employees.

3. Policy
- The manager submits the leaver form at least 10 working days before the last day.
- On the last day, all equipment and access cards are returned and accounts are disabled.
- Final pay, including unused annual leave, is settled within 30 days.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
