Title: Travel Cash Advance Policy (Remote-first Teams)
Document ID: FIN-0235
Department: Finance
Type: Policy
Version: 2.3
Effective date: 2024-10-17
Status: Active
Owner: Finance Shared Services
Applies to: Remote-first Teams employees
Related documents: FIN-0023, FIN-0031, FIN-0007

1. Purpose
This policy covers requesting a cash advance before a business trip.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Remote-first Teams employees.

3. Policy
- An advance of up to 5000 may be requested for trip costs not covered by the corporate card.
- The advance is requested at least 5 days before travel through the finance portal.
- The advance is reconciled against receipts within 14 days of returning; any unused amount is returned.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Finance Shared Services.

5. Contact
For questions, contact fin@t2.example.
