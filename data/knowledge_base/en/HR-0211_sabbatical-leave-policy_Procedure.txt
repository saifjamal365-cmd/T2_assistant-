Purpose
This policy covers extended unpaid leave for long-serving employees.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Employees with 3+ years of service may request up to 6 months of unpaid sabbatical leave.
- The request needs department head and HR approval at least 3 months in advance.
- Job protection is not guaranteed; the employee returns to an available role of similar level.
