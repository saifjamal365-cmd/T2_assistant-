Title: VPN and Remote Access Policy (Remote-first Teams)
Document ID: IT-0411
Department: Information Technology
Type: Policy
Version: 2.4
Effective date: 2025-02-11
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: Remote-first Teams employees
Related documents: HR-0063, IT-0015

1. Purpose
This policy explains how to connect to internal systems from outside the office.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Remote-first Teams employees.

3. Policy
- Internal systems are reachable from outside only through the approved VPN with MFA.
- Split tunnelling is disabled; all traffic goes through the VPN while connected.
- Sessions time out after 60 minutes of inactivity.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from IT Service Desk.

5. Contact
For questions, contact it@t2.example.
