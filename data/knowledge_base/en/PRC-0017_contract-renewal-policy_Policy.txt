Version: 3.1
Effective date: 2024-02-22
Status: Active
Owner: Procurement Office
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: FIN-0039, FIN-0047

1. Purpose
This policy makes sure supplier contracts are reviewed before they renew.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Procurement reviews every contract 3 months before its renewal date.
- Auto-renewal clauses are avoided; renewals are an active decision.
- The review checks price, usage, service quality and whether the need still exists.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Procurement Office.

5. Contact
For questions, contact prc@t2.example.
