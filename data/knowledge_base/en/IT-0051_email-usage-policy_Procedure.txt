Applies to: All employees
Related documents: IT-0031, IT-0103, LEG-0047

Purpose
This policy covers correct use of company email.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Company email is for business communication and is a company record.
- Do not auto-forward company email to a personal account.
- External emails with attachments over 10 MB should use the file-share link instead.
