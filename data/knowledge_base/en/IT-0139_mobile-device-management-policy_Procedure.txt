Applies to: All employees

Purpose
This policy covers enrolling phones and tablets that access company email or data.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Any device used for company email must be enrolled in the mobile device management (MDM) system.
- A lost or stolen enrolled device must be reported within 2 hours so it can be remotely wiped.
- Leaving the company or removing the device from MDM erases company data from it, not personal data.
