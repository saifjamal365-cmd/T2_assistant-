Purpose
This policy covers using electric-vehicle charging points at the office.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- EV charging bays are booked through the office app; a session is limited to 2 hours.
- Charging is free for employees; moving the car promptly once charged is expected.
- Non-EVs must not park in a charging bay, even briefly.
