1. Purpose
This policy explains how and when performance is reviewed.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Jeddah Office employees.

3. Policy
- Formal reviews take place 1 times a year, in June and December.
- Each review includes a self-assessment, manager feedback and, for senior roles, peer input.
- A rating of "needs improvement" for two cycles in a row triggers a performance plan.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
