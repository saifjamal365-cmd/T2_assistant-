Type: Procedure
Version: 3.3
Effective date: 2024-12-04
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: IT-0031, IT-0015

Purpose
This policy covers using personal phones or laptops for work.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Personal devices may access email and calendar only, and must be enrolled in mobile management.
- The device must have a screen lock and up-to-date operating system.
- The company can remotely wipe work data from a lost or stolen device.
