1. Purpose
This policy protects information left on desks and screens.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Remote-first Teams employees.

3. Policy
- Lock your screen whenever you leave your desk.
- Documents with client or personal data are locked away at the end of the day.
- Printouts left on the printer for more than 60 minutes are shredded.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from IT Service Desk.

5. Contact
For questions, contact it@t2.example.
