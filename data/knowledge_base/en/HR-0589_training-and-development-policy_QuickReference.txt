Quick reference - Training and Development - Quick Reference (Dubai Office).

Key points
- Each employee has an annual training budget of 8000 AED.
- Courses must be approved by the manager and be relevant to the current or next role.
- For a certification the company pays for, the employee agrees to stay 12 months or repay pro-rata.
