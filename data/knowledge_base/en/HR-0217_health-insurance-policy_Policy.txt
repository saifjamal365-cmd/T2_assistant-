1. Purpose
This policy explains medical insurance coverage for employees and dependents.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Coverage starts on the employee's first working day; no waiting period applies.
- Employees may add up to 4 dependents, with the premium shared as per the HR rate card.
- Claims are submitted through the insurer's app within 90 days of treatment.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
