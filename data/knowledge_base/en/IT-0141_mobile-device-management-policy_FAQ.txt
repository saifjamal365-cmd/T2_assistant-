Frequently asked questions about mobile device management - faq.

Q: Do I have to enrol my phone to get company email?
A: Yes, any device used for company email must be enrolled.

Q: What happens if I lose my enrolled phone?
A: Report it within 2 hours so it can be remotely wiped.
