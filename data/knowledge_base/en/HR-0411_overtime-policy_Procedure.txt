Purpose
This policy explains when overtime is paid and how it is approved.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Overtime must be approved in writing by the manager before it is worked.
- Overtime is paid at 1.25 times the normal hourly rate.
- Employees at grade D1 and above are not eligible for overtime pay.
