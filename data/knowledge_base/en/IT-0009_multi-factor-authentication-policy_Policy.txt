Title: Multi-Factor Authentication Policy
Document ID: IT-0009
Department: Information Technology
Type: Policy
Version: 3.0
Effective date: 2023-10-01
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: IT-0007, IT-0047

1. Purpose
This policy requires a second factor for sign-in.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- MFA is required for email, the VPN and any system holding client or financial data.
- The approved second factor is the authenticator app; SMS is allowed only as a fallback.
- Lost devices must be reported to the service desk within 2 hours.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from IT Service Desk.

5. Contact
For questions, contact it@t2.example.
