Purpose
This policy sets the probation period for new hires and how it is assessed.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- The probation period is 3 months from the start date.
- A review meeting is held at the midpoint and at the end of probation.
- During probation, either side may end the contract with 7 days' notice.
