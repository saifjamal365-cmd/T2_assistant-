1. Purpose
This policy sets the rules for account passwords.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Dubai Office employees.

3. Policy
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters and mix upper, lower, number and symbol.
- Passwords for systems with sensitive data must be changed every 180 days.
- Never share a password. IT staff will never ask for it.
- A suspected compromise must be reported and the password changed immediately.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from IT Service Desk.

5. Contact
For questions, contact it@t2.example.
