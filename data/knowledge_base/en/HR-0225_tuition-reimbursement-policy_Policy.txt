Related documents: HR-0175, HR-0127

1. Purpose
This policy covers reimbursement for job-related further education.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Up to 5000 AED per year is reimbursed for approved, job-related courses with a passing grade.
- The course must be approved by the manager before it starts; retroactive claims are not accepted.
- The employee agrees to stay 12 months after completion or repay the amount pro-rata.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
