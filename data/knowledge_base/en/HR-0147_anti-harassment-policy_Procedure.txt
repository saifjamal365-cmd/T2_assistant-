Effective date: 2023-09-19
Status: Active

Applies to: All employees
Related documents: HR-0143, HR-0159, LEG-0039

Purpose
This policy prohibits harassment and explains how to report it.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Harassment of any kind is prohibited and is treated as serious misconduct.
- Reports can be made to the manager, HR, or the confidential channel, and may be anonymous.
- Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is itself a violation.
