1. Purpose
This policy sets the notice period for resignation.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Jeddah Office employees.

3. Policy
- Employees give 2 weeks' written notice; grade M3 and above give 8 weeks.
- Notice may be shortened only with the manager's written agreement.
- An exit interview is offered before the last day.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
