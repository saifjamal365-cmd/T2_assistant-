Frequently asked questions about generative ai usage - faq.

Q: Can I paste client data into a public AI chatbot?
A: No, client and personal data must never be entered into a public AI tool.

Q: Can I use any AI tool I find useful?
A: No, only tools on the IT-approved list; new tools need IT review first.
