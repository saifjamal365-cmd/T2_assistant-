1. Purpose
This policy explains when employees can take unpaid leave.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Riyadh Office employees.

3. Policy
- Unpaid leave of up to 12 months may be requested after paid leave is exhausted.
- Approval requires the line manager and the department head to sign off.
- Benefits are paused during unpaid leave longer than 30 days.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
