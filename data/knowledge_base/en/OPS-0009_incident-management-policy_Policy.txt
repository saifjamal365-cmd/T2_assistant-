1. Purpose
This policy explains how service incidents are handled.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Incidents are logged with a severity from 1 (critical) to 4 (low).
- Severity 1 incidents get an incident commander and updates every 30 minutes.
- A written post-incident review is completed within 3 working days.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Operations Excellence.

5. Contact
For questions, contact ops@t2.example.
