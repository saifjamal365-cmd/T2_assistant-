1. Purpose
This policy explains restrictions that continue after an employee leaves.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Senior roles (grade M4 and above) may not join a direct competitor for 12 months after leaving.
- No employee may solicit T2 clients or staff for 12 months after leaving.
- The exact terms are set out in the employee's contract, not this policy alone.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Legal & Compliance Office.

5. Contact
For questions, contact leg@t2.example.
