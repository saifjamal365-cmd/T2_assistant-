Title: Anti-Bribery - Procedure
Document ID: LEG-0027
Department: Legal & Compliance
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.1
Effective date: 2023-09-11
Status: Active
Owner: Legal & Compliance Office
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: LEG-0023, LEG-0063, LEG-0039

Purpose
This policy prohibits bribery and improper payments.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Never offer, give, request or accept a bribe, in any form, to win business or influence a decision.
- Facilitation payments are not allowed, even where they are common.
- Any request for a bribe is reported to the Legal & Compliance Office immediately.
