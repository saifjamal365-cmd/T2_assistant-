Effective date: 2023-11-15

Applies to: Remote-first Teams employees
Related documents: FIN-0007

Purpose
This policy covers small cash payments held at each office.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Petty cash is for small items only, up to 200 AED per payment.
- Every payment needs a receipt and a signed voucher.
- The float is reconciled and topped up every week.
