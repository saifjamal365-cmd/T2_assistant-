Department: Information Technology
Type: Procedure
Version: 2.0
Effective date: 2023-10-06
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Applies to: Riyadh Office employees
Related documents: FAC-0031, IT-0047

Purpose
This policy covers internet access for visitors.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Guests are given access to a separate guest network only; it cannot reach internal systems.
- The guest Wi-Fi password is changed every 14 days and is available from reception.
- A staff member sponsoring a guest is responsible for that guest's network use.
