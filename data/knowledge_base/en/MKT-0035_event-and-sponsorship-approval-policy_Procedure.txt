Title: Event and Sponsorship Approval - Procedure
Document ID: MKT-0035
Department: Marketing & Communications
Type: Procedure
Version: 3.4
Effective date: 2024-09-18
Status: Active
Owner: Corporate Communications
Applies to: All employees
Related documents: FIN-0055, MKT-0023

Purpose
This policy covers approving company events and sponsorships.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Any event or sponsorship using the T2 name needs Corporate Communications approval before commitments are made.
- Requests are submitted at least 6 weeks ahead with a budget and objective.
- Sponsorships over 50000 SAR also need Finance approval.
