Applies to: All employees
Related documents: IT-0031, IT-0079

1. Purpose
This policy explains which software may be installed.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Only software approved and licensed by IT may be installed on company devices.
- Free or trial software may be used for evaluation for up to 14 days, then licensed or removed.
- Using pirated software is a serious policy violation.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from IT Service Desk.

5. Contact
For questions, contact it@t2.example.
