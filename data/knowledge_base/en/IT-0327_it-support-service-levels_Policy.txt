1. Purpose
This document sets response times for IT support tickets.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Dubai Office employees.

3. Policy
- Standard tickets are responded to within 2 business day(s).
- Critical issues, such as a full outage, are responded to within 4 hour(s) during business hours.
- Raise a ticket through the service desk portal, not by contacting staff directly.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from IT Service Desk.

5. Contact
For questions, contact it@t2.example.
