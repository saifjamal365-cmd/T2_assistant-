Purpose
This policy explains how to book flights and hotels for work trips.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- All travel is booked through the corporate travel portal, not directly with airlines or hotels.
- Economy class is standard; flights over 5 hours may be premium economy with manager approval.
- Hotels must not exceed the nightly cap for the destination shown in the portal.
- Book at least 7 days ahead where possible.
