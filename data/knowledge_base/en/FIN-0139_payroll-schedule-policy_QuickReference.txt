Quick reference - Payroll Schedule - Quick Reference (Dubai Office).

Key points
- Salaries are paid on the last day of each month, or the previous working day if that is a weekend or holiday.
- Changes to bank details must reach Payroll 5 working days before payday.
- Payslips are available in the HR system on payday.
