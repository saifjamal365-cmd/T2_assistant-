Quick reference - Press Enquiries - Quick Reference.

Key points
- Do not confirm, deny or comment on anything, even off the record.
- Take the journalist's name, outlet, question and deadline, and pass them to Corporate Communications the same day.
- Corporate Communications coordinates a single approved response.
