Quick reference - Client Escalation - Quick Reference.

Key points
- If the first point of contact cannot resolve an issue within 48 hours, it is escalated to the account manager.
- Issues with possible financial loss or a public complaint are flagged to the department head within 2 hours.
- Every escalation is logged in the CRM with a timeline of actions.
