Quick reference - On-Call - Quick Reference.

Key points
- On-call runs in 2-week rotations; the schedule is published a month ahead.
- An on-call allowance of 800 EGP per week is paid, plus overtime for call-outs.
- The on-call engineer acknowledges an alert within 15 minutes.
