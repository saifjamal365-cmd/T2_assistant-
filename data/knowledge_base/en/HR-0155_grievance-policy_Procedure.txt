Purpose
This policy explains how to raise a formal complaint at work.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Raise the issue informally with the manager first where possible.
- A formal grievance is submitted in writing and acknowledged within 5 working days.
- A hearing is held within 10 working days, and the outcome is given in writing.
