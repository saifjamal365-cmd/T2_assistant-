Applies to: All employees

Purpose
This policy covers shared kitchens and break areas.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Clean up after yourself; label personal food with your name and the date.
- The fridge is emptied every Thursday; anything unlabelled is thrown away.
- Report broken appliances to facilities.
