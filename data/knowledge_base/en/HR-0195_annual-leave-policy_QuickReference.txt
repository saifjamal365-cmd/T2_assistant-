Quick reference - Annual Leave - Quick Reference (Riyadh Office).

Key points
- Full-time employees are entitled to 25 paid annual leave days per year, accrued monthly.
- Requests must be submitted at least 3 working days in advance through the HR system.
- Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next year; any excess is forfeited.
- Leave cannot be combined with sick leave for the same period.
