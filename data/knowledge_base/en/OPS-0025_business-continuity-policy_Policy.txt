1. Purpose
This policy covers keeping key services running during a disruption.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Each team has a continuity plan naming its critical services and recovery time objective.
- Continuity plans are tested at least once a year.
- During a major disruption, the crisis team leads and communicates every 4 hour(s).

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from Operations Excellence.

5. Contact
For questions, contact ops@t2.example.
