Purpose
This policy sets the daily allowance for meals and incidentals while travelling.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- The per diem covers meals and small incidentals and removes the need for individual meal receipts.
- The daily rate is 250 EGP for domestic travel and 500 EGP for international travel.
- The per diem is not paid for days where all meals are provided.
