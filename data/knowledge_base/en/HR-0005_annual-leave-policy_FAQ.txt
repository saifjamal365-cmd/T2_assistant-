Frequently asked questions about annual leave - faq.

Q: How many annual leave days do I get?
A: Full-time employees get 25 paid days per year.

Q: How far in advance must I request leave?
A: At least 3 working days before the leave starts.

Q: Can I carry unused days to next year?
A: Yes, up to 7 days; the rest is lost.

More detail is in the matching policy document.
