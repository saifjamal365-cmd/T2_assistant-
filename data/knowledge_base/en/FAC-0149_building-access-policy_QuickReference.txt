Quick reference - Building Access - Quick Reference (Jeddah Office).

Key points
- Every employee has an access card. Do not let anyone "tailgate" in behind you.
- Lost cards are reported the same day; a replacement costs 25 EGP.
- After-hours access is logged and may need manager approval.

Owner: Facilities Management. Full policy: FAC policy set.
