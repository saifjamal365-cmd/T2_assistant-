Applies to: Remote-first Teams employees

Purpose
This policy covers secure and economical printing.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Printing needs a badge tap at the printer to release the job.
- Default printing is double-sided and black-and-white.
- Unreleased jobs are deleted after 4 hours.
