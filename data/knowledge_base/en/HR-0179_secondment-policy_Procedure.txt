Applies to: All employees
Related documents: HR-0191, HR-0095

Purpose
This policy covers temporary moves to another team or office.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- A secondment lasts 6 to 12 months and needs both managers to agree.
- The employee returns to their original role or an equivalent one at the end.
