Applies to: All employees
Related documents: HR-0111, HR-0143

Purpose
This policy explains the bonus for referring a candidate who is hired.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- A successful referral earns the employee a 2000 AED bonus, paid after the new hire completes probation.
- The role must be advertised internally; referrals for roles at grade M3 and above are not eligible.
- An employee may not refer a close relative.
