Related documents: HR-0119, LEG-0015

1. Purpose
This policy states how the company responds to reference requests.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees.

3. Policy
- Only HR may issue an official reference; it confirms title and dates of employment only.
- Personal references from managers must state that they are personal, not on behalf of T2.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
