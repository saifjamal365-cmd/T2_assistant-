Purpose
This policy encourages writing down and sharing how things are done.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Every recurring task should have a short written procedure in the shared knowledge base.
- Post-incident reviews and design decisions are written up and shared, not kept private.
- Pages unreviewed for 6 months are flagged for an owner to update or archive.
