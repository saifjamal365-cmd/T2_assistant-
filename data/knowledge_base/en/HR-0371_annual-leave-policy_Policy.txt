1. Purpose
This policy sets out how much paid annual leave employees receive and how to request it.

2. Scope
This policy applies to Jeddah Office employees.

3. Policy
- Full-time employees are entitled to 21 paid annual leave days per year, accrued monthly.
- Requests must be submitted at least 3 working days in advance through the HR system.
- Up to 7 unused days may be carried into the next year; any excess is forfeited.
- Leave cannot be combined with sick leave for the same period.

4. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations.

5. Contact
For questions, contact hr@t2.example.
