Quick reference - EV Charging - Quick Reference (Cairo Office).

Key points
- EV charging bays are booked through the office app; a session is limited to 2 hours.
- Charging is free for employees; moving the car promptly once charged is expected.
- Non-EVs must not park in a charging bay, even briefly.
