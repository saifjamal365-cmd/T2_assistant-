Applies to: Riyadh Office employees

Purpose
This policy covers incoming and outgoing post and parcels.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Personal parcels should not be sent to the office address.
- Outgoing business courier is booked through reception with a cost code.
- Reception holds parcels for 5 days before returning them to sender.
