Applies to: Riyadh Office employees
Related documents: HR-0119, HR-0135

Purpose
This policy sets the notice period for resignation.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Employees give 4 weeks' written notice; grade M4 and above give 12 weeks.
- Notice may be shortened only with the manager's written agreement.
- An exit interview is offered before the last day.
