Purpose
This policy covers scoring supplier performance over time.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- Key suppliers are scored every 3 months on quality, delivery and responsiveness.
- A score below 60 out of 100 triggers a formal improvement plan.
- Two consecutive low scores are referred to Procurement for a sourcing review.
