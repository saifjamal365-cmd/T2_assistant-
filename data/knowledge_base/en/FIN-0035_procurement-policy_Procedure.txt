Purpose
This policy explains how the company buys goods and services.

Steps
1. Check this document and any related policy before you start.
2. Prepare the information you need.
3. Submit the request in the correct system.
4. Wait for the required approval.
5. Keep a copy of the approval for your records.

Rules that apply
- A purchase request is raised before any commitment to a supplier.
- Purchases up to 5000 AED need one quote; above 5000 need 2 quotes.
- Purchases over 50000 AED go to the Procurement Committee.
